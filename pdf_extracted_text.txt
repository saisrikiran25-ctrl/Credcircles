=== PAGE 1 ===
A mobile-first platform that lets students and young adults form trusted “credit circles” (digital
SHGs), pool small contributions, lend to members and vetted outsiders, and earn modest
interest — with the platform acting as a connector + ledger, not a fund holder.
Young Indians (18–30) face a credit gap:
They need small, short-term loans for education, skills, emergencies, micro-businesses,
and lifestyle needs.
Traditional banks are slow, collateral-heavy, or unwilling for this segment.
Fintech lenders offer fast credit but at high interest and with little community
accountability.
Existing SHGs are mostly rural, women-focused, and offline; there’s no strong digital
equivalent for urban youth.
Gap: A trusted, digital, community-based credit system for students and young professionals
that is cheaper than fintechs and more accessible than banks.
A mobile app where users:
1. Form or join a Circle (e.g., college group, hostel group, friend circle, young professionals’
group).
2. Contribute small amounts to a logical group pool (tracked in-app; money stays in
members’ own bank accounts).
3. Decide collectively whom to lend to:
Within the group, or
To vetted external borrowers / other groups.
4. Lend via UPI directly from lender to borrower (no central fund holding).
5. Track everything in a transparent ledger:
