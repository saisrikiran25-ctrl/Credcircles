### Summary of Status
- **Phase 1 Mandatory Features**: 100% Implemented and verified.
- **Phase 2 / Stretch Horizons (Cross-sell, Auto-invest, B2B SaaS fallback, Option B license)**: Visible as styled, interactive cards or roadmap notices without cluttering core flows.
- **Out of Scope (By Design)**: Real fiat banking/escrow integrations and biometric KYC APIs (simulated behind clean service contracts per Working Rule #2).
